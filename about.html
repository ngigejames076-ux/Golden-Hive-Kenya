About Golden Hive Kenya

Golden Hive Kenya is a local business dedicated to provide pure, natural honey and hive products directly from Kenyan farmers.

We believe in quality, health, and supporting local beekeepers.

🍯 Our Mission

To deliver 100% pure, unprocessed honey from Wangige to every home in Kenya.

🌿 Our Values

Purity, Honesty, and Supporting local farmers. No sugar, no additives.

🐝 Why Us?

Direct from hive, tested quality, affordable prices. 500+ happy customers.

Powerful Products From The Hive

Our Story

Started in 2023 in Wangige, Kenya, after seeing many fake honeys in the market. We decided to partner with local beekeepers to bring real honey. From 1 hive to 20+ hives now.
